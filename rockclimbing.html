Rock Climbing

Rock climbing is a sport where someone uses their hands and feet to climb up a rock or an artificial climbing wall. Rock climbing is a difficult sport because you need to have a lot of strength. Rock climbers must know how to use ropes, carabiners and harnesses for their own safety.

Climbing is the activity of using one's hands, feet, or other parts of the body to ascend a steep topographical object that can range from the world's tallest mountains (e.g. the eight thousanders) to small boulders. Rock climbing would cost between £10-£15

Rock Climbing Trip Itinerary

Adventure Awaits!

  • Day 1: Arrival and Setup

    9:00 AM - Base Camp Arrival: Welcome and safety briefing.

    11:00 AM - Climbing Gear Check: Ensure all participants have the required gear.

    1:00 PM - Lunch at Base Camp: Healthy meal to fuel the day.

    3:00 PM - First Climb at Beginner Wall: Short, easy routes to get comfortable.

    7:00 PM - Campfire Dinner: Enjoy a relaxing meal and recap the day’s climb.

  • Day 2: Advanced Climbing and Exploration

    7:00 AM - Breakfast: Light breakfast and gear up for a day of adventure.

    9:00 AM - Rock Climbing Session: Start with a warm-up climb on a medium difficulty route.

    12:00 PM - Lunch on the Go: Packed sandwiches and snacks during a break at a scenic spot.

    2:00 PM - Explore the Summit: Hike to a panoramic viewpoint and enjoy breathtaking views.

    5:00 PM - Challenge Climb at Advanced Wall: Attempt a difficult climb to push your limits.

    7:30 PM - Evening Recap: Share climbing experiences and tips with fellow climbers over dinner.

  • Day 3: Climbing Challenge and Departure

    8:00 AM - Breakfast: Final breakfast at camp.

    9:00 AM - Climbing Challenge: A friendly competition to see who can climb the hardest route.

    12:00 PM - Pack Up: Pack all gear and prepare for departure.

    2:00 PM - Depart Base Camp: Bid farewell to the climbing location and head home.

Note: Please make sure to bring comfortable climbing shoes, appropriate clothing, and a lot of enthusiasm!
