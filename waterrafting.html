Water Rafting

Rafting and whitewater rafting are outdoor entertainment activities that use a boat or an unsinkable raft. It has different difficulty levels according to the current of the water in which the sport is done. It can be done solo but is more commonly done with groups and needs good teamwork to manage the water.

why you should go White Water Rafting?

It is a great way to get exercise and enjoy the outdoors. It is a challenging and exciting activity that can be enjoyed by people of all ages and skill levels. It is a great way to bond with friends and family. It allows you to see some of the most beautiful and remote places in the world. White water rafting would cost anywhere between £40 to £100 depending in your needs.

White Water Rafting Trip Itinerary

Prepare for an Adventure!

  • Day 1: Arrival and Introduction

    10:00 AM - Arrival at Base Camp: Meet at the base camp, check-in, and get settled.

    11:30 AM - Safety Briefing: Learn the essentials of rafting safety, paddling techniques, and emergency procedures.

    1:00 PM - Lunch at Base Camp: Enjoy a hearty meal with fellow adventurers.

    3:00 PM - Gear Up: Get your rafting gear, including life vests, helmets, and paddles.

    5:00 PM - Rafting Team Introduction: Meet your guide and rafting teammates, set expectations for the trip, and review the river course.

    7:00 PM - Welcome Dinner: Relax by the campfire, enjoy a delicious dinner, and bond with your rafting group.

  • Day 2: White Water Rafting Adventure

    6:30 AM - Breakfast at Camp: Fuel up with a nutritious breakfast.

    8:00 AM - Depart for the River: Head to the riverbank, get briefed again on paddling and the rapids you'll encounter.

    9:00 AM - Start Rafting: Begin your rafting journey through moderate rapids and scenic landscapes.

    12:00 PM - Lunch Break on Riverbank: Stop for a packed lunch by the river, relax, and enjoy the surroundings.

    1:00 PM - Continue Rafting: Tackle more challenging rapids as you move downstream.

    4:00 PM - Finish Rafting for the Day: End your rafting session at the next river access point. Relax and enjoy the view.

    6:00 PM - Return to Base Camp: Head back to base camp for some relaxation.

    7:30 PM - Dinner and Reflection: Share your rafting experiences with the group and enjoy a delicious meal around the campfire.

  • Day 3: Final Rafting Challenge and Departure

    7:00 AM - Breakfast at Camp: Start the day with a light breakfast.

    8:30 AM - Final Rafting Run: For the final leg, face the most exciting and challenging rapids of the trip!

    11:00 AM - Finish Rafting: Conclude the rafting portion of the trip and gather your belongings.

    12:00 PM - Lunch on the Riverbank: Enjoy a final packed lunch, reflect on the thrilling experience.

    2:00 PM - Pack Up and Departure: Return to camp, pack up, and depart for home with unforgettable memories.

Note: Be sure to bring sunscreen, water shoes, swimwear, and a spirit of adventure!
